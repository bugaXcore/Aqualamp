Header and settings menu: six options

Click the gear in each mockup to open its menu. Settings rows are placeholders, nothing is wired. Press Esc or click outside to close.

What the research says

  • Icon-only buttons need a name. Each gets an aria-label plus a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus, so nobody has to guess.
  • 44px touch targets. Icons stay 20px, the hit area is larger. Spacing between neighbours avoids mis-taps.
  • The gear is a disclosure. It carries aria-expanded and aria-controls, Esc closes it, and focus returns to the gear.
  • Match the container to the content. A popover suits a short list, a drawer or an in-header panel suits a long one, a bottom sheet suits one-handed phone use.
  • Keep Disconnect apart from Settings and tint it on hover only. It is the one destructive action in the bar.
  • Status stays a label, not a button. A dot plus a word is readable without colour alone.
  • Group settings with plain headings, put the most used first, and apply changes immediately with no Save button.
  • Respect reduced motion. Panels fade instead of sliding when the user asks for it.
